**Jordan (Lead Gen): first 30 days at $300/mo and ~8 hrs/wk**

No knowledge files yet beyond the README. Figures come from docs/03 §1-2 (Nov 2026, 🟡/🔴 vendor-reported, directional only). All data is sample data.

**Hypothesis:** with 8 hrs/week, time-based channels beat paid leads. Paid leads without a CRM and speed-to-lead system waste money.

**Ranked, with weekly hours**
1. **Sphere and referrals (about 3 hrs).** Load 150-300 contacts into the CRM, then make 50 touches a week, mostly calls and notes. Reported referral conversion is 14-40% 🔴, the highest of any channel. Record each contact's consent status. Don't send mass texts.
2. **Open houses on brokerage listings (about 3 hrs).** One a week at first, two once the rhythm holds. The sign-in sheet needs unchecked express-written-consent language for calls and texts. It's low cost and produces buyer leads in 1-3 months.
3. **Partner meetings (about 1 hr).** One lender, insurance agent, or condo manager a week. No payment for referrals (RESPA §8).
4. **Content (about 1 hr).** One ZIP or condo explainer a week, plus a monthly ZIP report. It's slow (6-18 months) but cheap, so start now.
5. **Paid test: not in month 1.** Hold the $300 as a reserve and as the test budget for later.

**When paid starts:** Day 31 or later, and only when all three gates are true:
- The CRM is live.
- First contact on a new lead takes under 5 minutes.
- A consent-capturing lead magnet has passed compliance review.

**Test design**
- Run Meta housing-category ads promoting one guide.
- Budget cap is $300/mo, with a minimum 60 days.
- Tag leads in the CRM as `meta-condo-guide`.
- **Stop:** no appointments after 60 days, or no signed buyer agreement by day 90.
- **Scale:** at least 2 appointments and cost per appointment under the target set with budget-analyst. Raise the budget by no more than 25% and re-check after 30 days.
- Small numbers will be noisy, so don't over-read them.
