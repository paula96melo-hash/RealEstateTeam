**Walter (Budget Analyst): sample month-1 plan.** All figures are a public demo example. Bracketed items are placeholders.

No knowledge files yet (`knowledge/budget-analyst/` has only its README).

**Assumptions**
| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Marketing budget | $300/mo (given) |
| Time | ~8 hrs/wk (given) |
| Savings | [Savings $] |
| Personal burn | [Personal $/mo] |
| Brokerage split / fees, dues, MLS | [Split %] / [Fees $/mo] |
| Income, months 1–6 | $0 (slow case). First closing often takes 3–6+ months 🟢 (docs/08 §1) |

**Month-1 split ($300)**
| Line | $ |
|---|---|
| Open house supplies (sign-in sheet with consent wording, refreshments) | 80 |
| Notes and postage to people the agent already knows (sphere) | 70 |
| CRM or tools (skip if the brokerage provides one) | 50 |
| Lender seminar share (costs split in proportion, per RESPA) | 40 |
| Unspent reserve, rolls forward | 60 |

No paid ads in month 1. The plan in docs/03 §2 runs paid tests only after the CRM and fast lead follow-up are working.

**Spend cap:** a hard cap of $300/mo. The first paid test comes in month 3 at the earliest. It is capped at $300, runs 60 days, and stops if it produces no appointments. That test replaces other spend; it does not add to it.

**Runway warning:**
Runway (months) = [Savings] ÷ ([Personal] + [Fees] + $300)

Hypothetical example: $18,000 ÷ ($4,000 + $500 + $300) = **about 3.75 months.** In the slow case the first check could arrive in month 6 or later, which leaves a gap of 2 or more months. Aim for 6–12 months of expenses saved (docs/08 §1). Running out of cash is risk #1.

**Escalations:** CPA for taxes (quarterly estimates, deductions). The broker or an attorney should confirm the lender co-marketing arrangement under RESPA.
