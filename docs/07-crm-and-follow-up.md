# 07 — CRM & Follow-Up Practices

> **As of:** November 2026. CRM comparisons online are mostly vendor-authored; no independent speed-to-lead benchmark exists. Pricing changes often — confirm before buying. Check what your **brokerage provides** first (many include BoldTrail/kvCORE, Lofty, or a proprietary CRM).

## 1. CRM options

| CRM | Best for | Notes | Approx. price (verify) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Follow Up Boss** | Solo agents & teams prioritizing lead routing, speed-to-lead, integrations with portals | Widely rated best pure CRM for routing; no native IDX site — pair with an IDX website | ~$58–$96/user/mo (sources vary) |
| **Lofty (formerly Chime)** | All-in-one: IDX site + CRM + AI follow-up | Strong automated nurture; vendor claims on AI | Varies by plan |
| **BoldTrail (formerly kvCORE)** | Brokerage-provided all-in-one | Often free/included via brokerage | Brokerage-dependent |
| **Sierra Interactive** | SEO/website-led teams running paid ads | Strong IDX websites | ~$300–$500+/mo + users |
| **Brokerage proprietary** (e.g., Compass, eXp/Real tools) | Agents at those brokerages | Free; less portable if you switch brokerages | Included |

**Recommendation for a new solo agent:** use the brokerage-provided CRM if it has texting, drips, and portal integrations; otherwise Follow Up Boss + a simple IDX site. **Data portability matters** — export your contacts regularly; your SOI is your business.

## 2. Required data model (for the agent team to implement)
- Contact: name, phones, emails, **consent records** (type, timestamp, source, form version, IP), DNC flags (federal, Florida, internal), language preference.
- Lead source & sub-source (e.g., "Open House – 123 Main St – 2026-12-06").
- Segment tags (B1–B7, S1–S7 from [02](02-buyer-and-seller-segments.md)), target ZIPs, price range, property type, timeline, financing status (pre-approved Y/N, lender).
- Stage: New → Attempted → Contacted → Nurture (A/B/C) → Appointment Set → **Buyer Agreement Signed** / Listing Agreement Signed → Showing/Active → Under Contract → Closed → Past Client.
- Key dates: home purchase anniversary, birthday (if offered), lease end, insurance renewal, homestead filing deadline (March 1).

## 3. Speed-to-lead & first contact
- Industry consensus: respond to new online leads within **5 minutes**; conversion drops sharply after the first hour (widely cited from lead-response studies; treat exact multipliers as directional).
- First touch sequence for a **consented** online lead:
  1. **0–5 min:** call. If no answer, text (only if consent covers texting) + email.
  2. **Day 1:** second call attempt at a different time of day.
  3. **Days 2–7:** daily touch alternating call/text/email (respect FTSA: ≤3 calls/24h on same subject, 8am–8pm).
  4. **Days 8–30:** 2–3 touches/week with value (listings matching criteria, ZIP report).
  5. **30+ days:** move to long-term nurture drip.
- Personalize: reference the property/guide they requested. Ask questions; don't pitch.

## 4. Long-term nurture (the money is in the follow-up)
| Lead type | Cadence | Content |
|---|---|---|
| A (0–3 mo timeline) | 2×/week personal | Listings, showings, lender intro |
| B (3–6 mo) | Weekly | Market updates, new listings, check-in calls |
| C (6–12+ mo) | 2×/month automated + monthly personal | ZIP report, guides, events |
| SOI | 33 touches/year model (e.g., 8 calls, 12 emails/newsletters, 4 notes, quarterly events/pop-bys) | Personal + value |
| Past clients | Monthly email, quarterly call, annual home-value review, closing anniversary | Equity updates, homestead/Amendment 3 reminders, insurance renewal tips |

## 5. Automation & AI — guardrails
- Automated texts require **prior express written consent**; honor any revocation keyword immediately (CRM should auto-suppress "STOP," "quit," "cancel," "unsubscribe," "end," "revoke," "opt out").
- AI-drafted messages: human review for fair-housing language and accuracy; never let AI give legal/tax/insurance advice.
- AI voice calling = "artificial voice" under TCPA (FCC 2024 ruling) — requires prior express consent.
- Log every touch automatically for compliance evidence.

## 6. Open house follow-up script framework
1. Same day: thank-you text/email (if consented) + the property's disclosure/fact sheet.
2. Next day: call — "What did you think? What would make it a perfect fit?" → offer search setup.
3. Before touring any home with them: **sign a written buyer agreement** (NAR settlement requirement).

## 7. Weekly CRM review ritual (30–45 min)
- Every new lead contacted? Every A-lead has a next task?
- Overdue tasks cleared; stale leads re-tiered.
- Lead source report: which sources produced appointments this month?
- Pipeline: projected closings & GCI for next 90 days.

## 8. Benchmarks
- Portal leads convert ~0.4–3% over 12–24 months; referrals ~14–40% (vendor ranges, 🔴). Plan for long nurture on paid leads.
- Typical new-agent target: 1 appointment per ~10–20 meaningful conversations (rule of thumb, not verified).
