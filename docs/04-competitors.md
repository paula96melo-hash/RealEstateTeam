# 04 — Competitive Landscape

> **As of:** November 2026. Brokerage market-share data for Palm Beach County is largely paywalled (The Real Deal rankings) — figures below are partial. **Before publishing competitor comparisons, re-verify.** Never make false or unverifiable comparative claims in ads (FREC 61J2-10.025; Fla. Stat. 475.42).

## 1. Market structure
- **Association / MLS:** West Palm Beach agents typically belong to **Broward, Palm Beaches & St. Lucie Realtors (RWorld)** — formed from the 2017 merger of the Realtors Association of the Palm Beaches and Greater Fort Lauderdale Realtors — and use **BeachesMLS**, which reportedly has ~36,000 agent users across southeast Florida (data-share with Miami MLS). That means **tens of thousands of licensees compete for the same listings**. 🟡
- **Concentration:** The Real Deal reported the top 25 agent teams in Palm Beach County closed **$6.1B** in on-market deals in 2024 — production is highly concentrated at the top, especially in luxury. 🟡
- **Luxury is boutique-friendly:** TRD (Jul 2025) notes the top Palm Beach County broker isn't at Douglas Elliman, Corcoran or Compass; independent boutiques (e.g., Premier Estate Properties, Echo Fine Properties) rank highly. 🟡

## 2. Competitor archetypes

| Archetype | Examples in market (not exhaustive) | Strengths | Weaknesses / openings for a new agent |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Luxury national brands** | Douglas Elliman, Corcoran, Compass, Sotheby's International Realty affiliates, Brown Harris Stevens | Brand with NE relocators, global referral networks, marketing budgets | Focus on $2M+; less attention to entry-level, condo-investor, and Spanish/Creole-speaking segments |
| **Luxury boutiques** | Premier Estate Properties, Echo Fine Properties, others | Deep Palm Beach Island relationships, top-agent concentration | Narrow price band |
| **Large regional / franchise** | Keller Williams (Reserve Palm Beach, Palm Beach Central), Coldwell Banker, Keyes Company, Lang Realty, RE/MAX, Illustrated Properties | Training, lead programs, volume of listings for open houses | Agent saturation — internal competition |
| **Cloud / high-split brokerages** | eXp, Real, LPT, Fathom | Low fees, revenue share | Less local mentorship; agent must self-generate leads |
| **Mega-teams / lead-buying teams** | Top RealTrends teams (e.g., Compass and KW teams in WPB) | Portal lead spend, ISAs, systems | Leads go to rainmaker; new agents on teams get high splits taken |
| **Discount / flat-fee & iBuyer-style** | Redfin, Houwzer-type flat-fee models, Opendoor | Price | Less local service; seller-education gap |
| **Builder in-house sales** | Toll Brothers, DiVosta/Pulte, GL Homes, Kolter, Minto (Westlake) | Control the new-construction buyer at first visit | Buyers lack representation — register-and-accompany opportunity |
| **FSBO / for-sale-by-owner platforms** | Zillow FSBO, flat-fee MLS | Low cost | Pricing/disclosure risk → education opportunity |

## 3. How competitors market (observed patterns)
- **Content:** monthly market reports by ZIP/city (PBP Real Estate, Momentum, Echo publish frequently-updated data pages that rank on Google). These sites dominate "West Palm Beach housing market" search results — evidence that **data-rich, frequently updated local pages** win SEO here.
- **Condo-risk content** is a hot blog theme in 2026 across local teams — a crowded but valuable topic; differentiate with building-specific, document-based analysis.
- **Luxury:** print magazines, Palm Beach social events, PR placements, video tours.
- **Teams:** heavy Zillow/Realtor.com spend plus ISAs.

## 4. Positioning options for a newly licensed agent
1. **"WPB Condo Due-Diligence Agent"** — reads SIRS/milestone reports, budgets, minutes; publishes neutral building checklists. Underserved, high trust.
2. **First-time buyer specialist (33409/33411/33413/33415)** with lender + DPA expertise, bilingual content if applicable.
3. **Northeast relocation guide** — YouTube neighborhood series (Northwood, El Cid, SoSo, Flamingo Park, downtown) to feed luxury agents/teams referrals early and own the niche later.
4. **Hyper-local farm** — one neighborhood, 300–500 homes, consistent mail + events.

## 5. Brokerage selection criteria (for the new agent)
| Criterion | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Mentorship / training program | First 6–12 months determine survival |
| Access to listings for open houses | #1 low-cost buyer lead source |
| Lead programs (Zillow Preferred, ReadyConnect, in-house) & their split | Determines cost of leads |
| Compliance support (ad review, TCPA/FTSA tools) | Avoid violations |
| Tech stack (CRM, IDX site) & cost | See [07](07-crm-and-follow-up.md) |
| Split, caps, desk/E&O fees | Cash-flow runway |
| Team policy (team names under 61J2-10.026) | Branding freedom |
