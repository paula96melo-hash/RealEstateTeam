# 03 — Lead Sources

> **As of:** November 2026. Cost and conversion figures are mostly vendor/blog-reported — treat as directional (🟡/🔴) unless marked 🟢. Every outbound channel (calls, texts, emails) must pass the consent rules in [05](05-compliance-advertising-fair-housing.md).

## 1. Channel economics at a glance

| Channel | Typical cost | Reported conversion (lead → close) | Time to first deal | New-agent verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Sphere of influence (SOI) & referrals** | Time + small gifts/events | **14–40%** (referral leads) 🔴 | 1–6 months | **Start here.** Cheapest, highest-converting |
| **Open houses (hosting others' listings)** | Signs, refreshments, ~$0–$100/event | Moderate; strong for buyer leads | 1–3 months | **Core activity** — broker listings are available to new agents |
| **Zillow Premier Agent (pay-per-lead)** | ~$223/lead major metro, ~$139 non-major; $50/ZIP minimum 🟡 | **0.4–3%** portal leads 🔴 | 3–9 months | Only with strict speed-to-lead system and budget runway |
| **Zillow Preferred (formerly Flex, renamed Oct 15, 2025)** | No upfront; **~15–40% referral fee at close** 🟡 | Same traffic, pay-at-close | Requires invitation/team access | Usually accessed via a team/brokerage that has it |
| **Realtor.com ReadyConnect Concierge** | No upfront; **~28–40% referral fee at close** (not officially published) 🔴 | Pre-qualified via call center | Varies | Good via brokerages that participate |
| **Brokerage-provided leads / floor time** | Higher split or referral fee to broker | Varies | Immediate | Ask during brokerage selection |
| **Facebook/Instagram ads** (Special Ad Category: Housing) | **$10–$80/lead** digital ads 🟡 | Low (often <1%); long nurture | 6–12 months | Test small ($300–$500/mo) only with CRM nurture in place |
| **Google Search / Local Services Ads** | Higher CPL than social; higher intent | Moderate | 3–9 months | Later stage; LSA requires reviews & verification |
| **SEO / YouTube neighborhood content** | Time; ~$0–$300/mo tools | High intent, slow build | 6–18 months | **Long-term moat** — start immediately, low cost |
| **Direct mail (absentee owners, just-sold, farm)** | ~$0.60–$1.50/piece incl. postage (estimate) | Low per piece, compounding | 6–12 months | Pick one farm of 300–500 homes |
| **Expireds / FSBOs prospecting** | Data subscription + time | Moderate for listings | 1–6 months | Only with DNC scrubbing & FTSA compliance |
| **Agent-to-agent referrals (out-of-state)** | 25–35% referral fee paid out | High | Varies | Build NE relocation network (NY/NJ/CT agents) |
| **Builder/new-construction co-op** | Free; must register client at 1st visit | High per-buyer value | 1–3 months | Accompany buyer on first visit or lose co-op |
| **Professional partners** (lenders, CPAs, divorce/probate attorneys, insurance agents, property managers) | Time; RESPA limits on things of value | High | 3–12 months | Strong; **no payment for referrals** — RESPA §8 |
| **Community/niche** (HOA events, language communities, nonprofits) | Time | High trust | 3–12 months | Strong for B2/B4 segments |

Sources for figures: Zillow CPL and Preferred rename per Jamil Academy & Prime Listing Leads summaries; referral/portal conversion ranges per RealScout, Perspective, Conversion Realtor (vendor research). NAR 2026 surveyed CPL claims (~$503 average) could not be verified against NAR.

## 2. Recommended 90-day lead plan for a newly licensed agent (low budget)

**Weeks 1–2 (foundation)**
- Load full SOI (target 150–300 contacts) into CRM; tag A/B/C. Record consent status for each.
- Personal announcement: letter/email + phone calls (not mass texts) to SOI.
- Claim Google Business Profile only if brokerage policy allows (brokerage name must appear — see [05](05-compliance-advertising-fair-housing.md)); set up Zillow/Realtor.com/Homes.com agent profiles.

**Weeks 2–12 (repeatable weekly rhythm)**
- 2 open houses/week on brokerage listings (Sat/Sun), each with a sign-in that captures **express written consent** for texts/calls.
- 50 SOI touches/week (calls, handwritten notes, coffee).
- 1 YouTube neighborhood/market video + 3–5 short-form clips/week (see [06](06-social-media-strategy.md)).
- 1 monthly email market report by ZIP.
- 1 partner meeting/week (lender, insurance agent, condo association manager, attorney).
- Host 1 first-time buyer seminar/month with a lender (co-marketing must split costs fairly per RESPA).

**Paid tests (only after CRM + speed-to-lead are live)**
- $300–$500/mo Meta housing-category lead ads promoting a downloadable guide (e.g., "2027 WPB Condo Buyer's Due-Diligence Checklist", "What Amendment 3 Means for Your Homestead").
- Measure: cost per lead, cost per appointment, cost per signed buyer agreement, cost per closing. Kill anything without appointments in 60 days.

## 3. Lead magnets that fit the 2026–27 market
- Condo building due-diligence checklist / "assessment watch" reports (factual, building-level).
- Amendment 3 homestead exemption explainer (with attorney/appraiser citations).
- Insurance cost guide (co-created with licensed insurance agent).
- Monthly ZIP-level market reports (33401, 33405, 33407, 33409, 33411).
- Relocation guide for Northeast buyers (commute, PBIA flights, Brightline, schools via official data links).
- First-time buyer guide with down-payment assistance programs (Florida Housing, county programs — verify current availability).

## 4. KPIs to track
| Funnel stage | Metric |
|---|---|
| Activity | Conversations/week, open houses, content pieces |
| Lead | New leads by source, cost per lead |
| Contact | Speed to first contact (target < 5 min), contact rate |
| Appointment | Appointments set/held, signed buyer agreements, listing presentations |
| Close | Pending, closed, GCI, cost per closing by source |
