# 06 — Social Media & Content Strategy

> **As of:** November 2026. Most social-media "statistics" in the industry are vendor-sourced and conflicting (🔴). The better-sourced finding: **social is a real but secondary first-contact channel** — NAR data puts buyers' first contact with their agent via social media at ~3%, while ~22% of sellers say their agent used social networking to market the home. Social's main job is **credibility + staying top-of-mind with your sphere**, and long-form YouTube/SEO for **search-driven relocation buyers**.
> All content must pass [05 §8](05-compliance-advertising-fair-housing.md#8-pre-publish-compliance-checklist-for-every-marketing-asset). Brokerage name in every profile and on property posts.

## 1. Platform roles

| Platform | Role | Audience fit (segments from [02](02-buyer-and-seller-segments.md)) | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Instagram** | Primary brand & SOI top-of-mind | B2, B1, B5, sellers | Reels (vertical), carousels (market stats, checklists), Stories (open houses, day-in-life) |
| **YouTube** | Search engine — long-term lead moat | B1 relocators, B4, B6 | 8–15 min neighborhood tours, "living in WPB" explainers, market updates; Shorts repurposed |
| **Facebook** | Local groups, older demographics, events, Special-Category ads | B4, S2, SOI | Event pages, open-house posts, community group participation (follow group rules — no spam) |
| **TikTok** | Reach / first-time buyer education | B2, B7 | Fast explainers, myth-busting |
| **LinkedIn** | Finance/corporate relocators, professional partners | B1, partners | Market commentary, relocation insights |
| **Google Business Profile** | Local search, reviews | All | Posts, Q&A, reviews (brokerage name required) |
| **Nextdoor** | Hyper-local farm presence | Sellers in farm | Neighborhood updates (follow platform business rules) |

## 2. Content pillars (aligned to 2026–27 local themes)

1. **Neighborhood guides (evergreen):** Northwood/Old Northwood, El Cid, Flamingo Park, SoSo, Downtown/Rosemary Square, Grandview Heights, Prospect Park, Royal Palm Beach, Westlake. Describe amenities, architecture, parks, restaurants, commute — **never who lives there** or school/safety judgments.
2. **Market data:** monthly ZIP reports (SF vs. condo), rate updates, "what $500k buys in WPB," with source and date on screen.
3. **Condo due-diligence series:** how to read a SIRS, milestone inspection, budget; what special assessments mean; financing implications.
4. **Policy explainers:** Amendment 3 homestead exemption, insurance (with licensed partner), flood disclosure, NAR buyer agreements ("why you sign before touring").
5. **Relocation:** NE → WPB guides; PBIA, Brightline, cost comparisons (cite sources).
6. **Process education:** first-time buyer steps, down-payment assistance, seller prep, net sheets.
7. **Personal/brand:** behind-the-scenes, local businesses, community involvement — authenticity outperforms polish (Florida Realtors 2026).
8. **Proof:** client stories/testimonials (with written permission), just-listed/just-sold (with listing broker permission for others' listings — never imply you sold a listing you didn't).

## 3. Cadence (sustainable for a solo new agent)

| Weekly | Monthly |
|---|---|
| 3 Instagram Reels/TikToks (median real estate brands post ~3 Reels/week per Dash Social benchmark) | 1 ZIP market report (carousel + email + blog) |
| 1 carousel | 2–4 YouTube long-form videos |
| Daily Stories when active (open houses, tours) | 1 live Q&A (e.g., with lender or insurance agent) |
| 1 YouTube video (or 2/month minimum) | Content audit: which posts produced DMs, calls, appointments |
| 2–3 Facebook group contributions | |

**Batching workflow:** film one neighborhood on one morning → 1 long YouTube video + 5–8 Shorts/Reels + 1 blog post + 1 carousel + 1 email section.

## 4. YouTube/SEO playbook (highest long-term ROI for relocation)
- Titles match search intent: "Living in West Palm Beach 2027: Pros & Cons," "Northwood West Palm Beach Neighborhood Tour," "West Palm Beach Condo Special Assessments Explained."
- Local data pages (like PBP Real Estate and Momentum sites that dominate Google for WPB market searches) show **frequently-updated, data-rich pages rank**. Build a blog/IDX site with monthly-updated ZIP pages.
- Every video description: brokerage name, license disclosure, EHO statement, link to lead magnet with compliant consent form.

## 5. Converting social to leads
- Each post has one CTA: "DM 'CONDO' for the checklist" → automation delivers guide → **consent captured on form** before any texting.
- Use link-in-bio landing page tied to CRM; tag lead source "IG-Reel-[topic]".
- Respond to DMs/comments within the hour during business hours.
- Retarget engaged audiences only within platform housing-category rules.

## 6. Metrics
- Leading: posting consistency, saves/shares, profile visits, DMs started.
- Lagging (what matters): leads by social source, appointments, signed buyer/listing agreements, closings attributable to social/YouTube.
- Engagement benchmarks vary wildly (0.3%–4%) by source — **benchmark against your own baseline**.

## 7. Don'ts
- No posting other agents' listings as if your own; no MLS photos without permission/IDX compliance.
- No "perfect for families/young professionals/retirees" captions.
- No unsubstantiated superlatives ("top agent," "#1") or invented stats.
- No AI-generated fake property images presented as real; disclose virtual staging.
- No buying followers/reviews; FTC endorsement rules apply to testimonials and influencer collabs (disclose material connections).
