# 02 — Buyer & Seller Segments

> **As of:** November 2026. Segments are synthesized from the market data in [01](01-market-and-zip-trends.md), NAR's 2025 Profile of Home Buyers and Sellers, and local coverage. Treat sizing as qualitative unless a number is cited.
> **Fair-housing guardrail:** Segments below are defined by *housing need, price point, property type, transaction situation, and geography of origin for marketing reach* — never by protected class. Targeting and ad copy must follow [05](05-compliance-advertising-fair-housing.md). Do not describe who "belongs" in a neighborhood.

## National context (NAR 2025 Profile, transactions Jul 2024–Jun 2025) 🟢
- First-time buyers: **21%** of buyers — record low. Median first-time buyer age: **40** (record high). Repeat buyer median age: **62**.
- Median down payment: **10%** first-time, **23%** repeat.
- **88%** of buyers used an agent or broker.
- Only ~**3%** of buyers' first contact with their agent came via social media; referrals and past relationships dominate (see [03](03-lead-sources.md)).

## Seller segments

| # | Segment | Trigger | Pain points | Approach |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | **Condo owners facing assessments** | SIRS/milestone-driven special assessments ($10k–$100k+ reported), rising HOA dues | Price discounts, buyer financing limits, emotional stress | Building-specific market analyses; honest pricing; target buildings with known assessments via neutral, factual content |
| S2 | **Long-tenure homesteaders (equity-rich, rate-locked)** | Downsizing, life events, Amendment 3 education | Losing low rate & Save Our Homes cap; capital-gains concerns | Portability + Amendment 3 explainers; net-proceeds calculators; partner with CPA/attorney |
| S3 | **Estate / probate / divorce sellers** | Life events | Need speed, discretion, multiple decision-makers | Attorney referral relationships; sensitive, compliant outreach |
| S4 | **Out-of-state owners & landlords** | Insurance costs, tenant fatigue, tax change for non-homestead | Remote management, insurance renewals | Absentee-owner direct mail (check DNC rules for calls/texts) |
| S5 | **Expired / withdrawn listings** | 61+ DOM and rising; overpriced listings | Frustration with prior agent | Data-driven re-pricing pitch; must not interfere with active exclusive listings |
| S6 | **FSBOs** | Saving commission | Exposure, pricing, legal risk | Value content; respect DNC/FTSA before calling/texting |
| S7 | **Insurance-pressured owners** | Citizens depopulation/takeouts, premiums ~$6.3k avg | Affordability of holding | Insurance-education content with licensed insurance agent partner |

## Recommended starting focus for a newly licensed agent
1. **B2 + B7** (first-time & renters→owners in 33409/33411/33413/33415) for early volume and reviews.
2. **B3 + S1** (condo specialist) as a differentiating niche few agents do well.
3. **B1** via content (YouTube neighborhood guides of 33401/33405/33407) to build long-term luxury/relocation pipeline.
4. **Sphere of influence** across all segments (see [03](03-lead-sources.md)).
