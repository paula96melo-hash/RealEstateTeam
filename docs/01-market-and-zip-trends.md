## 4. Demand drivers

- **"Wall Street South" / finance migration.** Reports cite 250+ financial firms establishing a presence in West Palm Beach since 2020, with Goldman Sachs and JPMorgan expanding locally 🔴 (promotional/press sources; directionally consistent with Bisnow and The Real Deal coverage). Drives luxury downtown condo and South End demand.
- **Northeast relocators.** Buyers from NYC, Westchester, Long Island, Fairfield County; PBIA proximity is a cited deciding factor 🟡.
- **Brightline** rail connects WPB to Fort Lauderdale, Miami and Orlando — supports car-light downtown living; no hard data linking it to buyer volume 🔴.
- **New property-tax regime.** Florida **Amendment 3 passed Nov 3, 2026 (~62%)**: raises the non-school homestead exemption to **$150,000 in 2027 and $250,000 in 2028**, and lowers the cap on annual assessment increases for non-homestead property 🟢 (CBS12, News4Jax, Bay News 9). Implications: stronger incentive to homestead; possible deeper "lock-in" for existing homesteaders (Save Our Homes portability still matters); local-government budget pressure may show up in fees/millage. **This is a strong content topic for Q1 2027.**
- **Insurance easing slightly.** Citizens cut Palm Beach rates ~11.7–11.9% for 2026; county average premium H1 2026 ≈ **$6,323** (−1.4%), still among Florida's highest 🟢 (Citizens, WPTV).

## 5. Condo market — the split

The single most important local nuance for 2026–27:

- Post-Surfside laws (SB 4-D, SB 154, **HB 913 (2025)**) require milestone inspections and Structural Integrity Reserve Studies (SIRS) for buildings with **3+ habitable stories**; SIRS deadline for older associations was extended to **Dec 31, 2025**. HB 913 also allows associations to borrow for reserves and (per secondary sources) pause some reserve funding through 2028 with owner vote 🟢 (Florida Senate bill summary).
- Result: a **two-tier market**. Buildings with completed milestone inspection, funded SIRS, and no pending assessment hold value; older buildings with pending assessments trade at a discount. Brokerage blogs report assessments of **$10k to $100k+ per unit**, including a reported $12M assessment on a 140-unit WPB building that led to a bulk buyout offer 🔴.
- Lending: several sources reference new condo mortgage/eligibility rules (Fannie/Freddie "unavailable/non-warrantable" building lists) affecting financeability 🔴 — verify current agency guidance.
- **Opportunity:** a newly licensed agent who becomes the "condo due-diligence" specialist (reading SIRS, milestone reports, budgets, minutes, assessment history) has a defensible niche.

## 6. What agents should track monthly

1. BeachesMLS: closed sales, median price, DOM, months of supply, list-to-sale ratio — split SF vs. condo, by target ZIP.
2. Freddie Mac PMMS (weekly).
3. Citizens and private-carrier rate filings for Palm Beach County.
4. Amendment 3 implementation rules from the Legislature / Property Appraiser.
5. Condo building watchlist: milestone/SIRS status, special assessments, DBPR filings.
