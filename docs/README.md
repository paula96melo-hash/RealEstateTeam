## Ten things every agent should know
1. **Market:** rates back above 7% (7.28% on Nov 19, 2026); Palm Beach County volume down ~8% YoY; single-family prices holding/rising (WPB SF median ~$625k in Sep 2026, up from $540k), but **condos are a buyer's market** (~8.5 months supply).
2. **Condo split:** buildings with completed milestone inspections/funded SIRS hold value; others face big special assessments. Condo due diligence is a differentiating niche.
3. **Amendment 3 passed (Nov 3, 2026):** non-school homestead exemption rises to $150k (2027) and $250k (2028). Major content and seller-conversation topic.
4. **Insurance** remains the top cost objection (~$6.3k avg premium), though Citizens rates fell ~12% in 2026.
5. **Demand:** high end fueled by NE/finance relocators (downtown, South End); volume in entry-level 33409/33411/33413/33415; countywide population growth has nearly stalled.
6. **Best early lead sources:** sphere of influence, open houses on brokerage listings, partner referrals, and long-term YouTube/SEO. Paid portal leads ($139–$223 each, low conversion) only after follow-up systems work.
7. **Every ad needs the brokerage's licensed name** (Rule 61J2-10.025); team names can't be bigger than the brokerage name (61J2-10.026).
8. **Fair housing:** Palm Beach County adds age, marital status, sexual orientation, and gender identity/expression to the federal classes. Describe properties, not people. Housing ads on Meta/Google must use special-category targeting.
9. **Texts/calls:** prior express written consent, DNC scrubbing (federal + Florida), 8am–8pm, max 3 calls per 24 hours, honor opt-outs (Florida's mini-TCPA). Signed buyer agreement before any tour.
10. **Biggest risk for a new agent** is running out of money before the pipeline matures; the most expensive mistakes are TCPA/FTSA and fair-housing violations.

## How agents should use this research
- **Freshness:** market figures are snapshots. Re-pull BeachesMLS data monthly and rates weekly before publishing numbers. Always state source and date in client-facing content.
- **Confidence markers:** 🟢 primary/official source · 🟡 MLS-derived data reported by a brokerage site · 🔴 single, promotional, or unverified source. Don't present 🔴 figures as fact.
- **Compliance gate:** every outward-facing asset passes [05 §8](05-compliance-advertising-fair-housing.md#8-pre-publish-compliance-checklist-for-every-marketing-asset) and, where brokerage policy requires, broker approval.
- **Not legal advice:** escalate legal, tax, and insurance questions to the supervising broker, a Florida attorney, a CPA, or a licensed insurance agent.
