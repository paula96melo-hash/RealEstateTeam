# 08 — Biggest Risks

> **As of:** November 2026. Ranked by likelihood × impact for a **newly licensed** West Palm Beach agent. Each risk lists early-warning signals and mitigations for the agent team to monitor.

| 2 | TCPA / Florida FTSA violations from texting/calling | Medium–High | Severe (statutory damages per message; class actions) | Legal |
| 3 | Fair-housing violations in ads, AI copy, targeting, or conversations | Medium | Severe | Legal/reputational |

## Detail & mitigations

### 1. Cash runway / new-agent attrition
- NAR membership has declined from its 1.6M peak (Oct 2022) to ~1.44M (June 2026); agents with <1 year experience are only ~11% of membership; median Realtor gross income ~$59,200 (2025) 🟢. The popular "75% fail in year one / 87% in five years" stats are **unverified** — but attrition is real.
- First closing often takes 3–6+ months; commissions arrive at closing.
- **Mitigate:** 6–12 months of personal expenses saved; low-cost lead sources first (SOI, open houses); brokerage with mentorship; weekly activity KPIs ([03 §4](03-lead-sources.md#4-kpis-to-track)).

### 2. TCPA / FTSA exposure
- Florida's mini-TCPA has been a plaintiff-bar favorite. Texting purchased lists or unscrubbed numbers is the single most expensive mistake.
- **Mitigate:** consent capture at every form; DNC (federal + Florida) scrubbing; 8am–8pm; ≤3 calls/24h; instant opt-out handling; no autodialing/AI voice without consent. See [05 §4](05-compliance-advertising-fair-housing.md#4-calls-texts--email--consent-rules).

### 3. Fair housing
- Palm Beach County protects more classes than federal law (age, marital status, sexual orientation, gender identity/expression). AI copy tools and "family-friendly" phrasing are common pitfalls; Meta/Google targeting restrictions apply.
- **Mitigate:** pre-publish checklist; property-not-people descriptions; refer school/crime questions to official sources; annual fair-housing training.

### 4. Rates & volume
- 30-yr fixed ~7.28% (Nov 19, 2026) vs. 6.26% a year earlier; PBC closed sales −8% YoY (Oct 2026). SF DOM in WPB rose to 61.
- **Mitigate:** lender partnerships (buydowns, ARMs, assumables, DPA); seller-concession strategies; nurture long-timeline leads; listings over buyers where possible.

### 5. Condo distress
- 8.5 months condo supply; most PBC condo sales close under list; special assessments of $10k–$100k+ reported; SIRS/milestone compliance determines value and financeability.
- **Mitigate:** obtain and read association docs, SIRS, milestone reports, budgets and minutes early; confirm lender/warrantability before writing offers; set seller expectations honestly; refer legal questions to a condo attorney. Liability risk if agent misrepresents building condition.

### 6. Insurance
- Average PBC premium ~$6,323 (H1 2026), among Florida's highest; Citizens depopulation moved many owners to private carriers (Palm Beach Citizens policies −65% YoY) 🟢. Roof age and 4-point/wind-mitigation results drive insurability.
- **Mitigate:** get insurance quotes during inspection period; partner with licensed insurance agents (no RESPA-violating referral payments); educate buyers early.

### 7. FREC advertising
- Brokerage name missing from social posts/profiles is the most common violation; team names larger than brokerage name; unverified "#1" claims.
- **Mitigate:** [05 §8](05-compliance-advertising-fair-housing.md#8-pre-publish-compliance-checklist-for-every-marketing-asset) checklist; templates with brokerage branding locked in.

### 8. Commission & buyer-agreement friction
- Written buyer agreements required before touring; compensation negotiable and often no longer offered by sellers; consumers more price-sensitive.
- **Mitigate:** a clear value presentation and buyer consultation; know the Florida Realtors forms cold; be ready to negotiate compensation in offers.

### 9. Paid lead burn
- Portal leads ~$139–$223 each with ~0.4–3% conversion; pay-at-close programs take 15–40%.
- **Mitigate:** only buy leads once speed-to-lead and nurture are proven; track cost per closing; cap monthly spend.

### 10. Hurricanes, flood & climate
- Season Jun 1–Nov 30; binding insurance freezes during named storms can delay closings; flood disclosure law (Fla. Stat. 689.302) requires seller disclosure.
- **Mitigate:** contract hurricane clauses; FEMA flood map checks; elevation certificates; contingency planning for closings; content on preparedness.

### 11. Wire fraud & deepfakes
- Real estate closings are top targets for business email compromise; AI voice/video impersonation is rising.
- **Mitigate:** written wire-fraud warnings at contract; verify wiring instructions by phone with known title-company numbers; MFA on email/CRM; never send wire instructions by email.

### 12. Population/migration
- PBC added only ~1,600 residents in 2024–25 (vs. 13,800 prior year); domestic migration turned negative in 2023–24 🟢. WPB city still growing, and NE/finance relocation continues at the high end.
- **Mitigate:** diversify across segments (local first-time buyers, move-ups, sellers) rather than relying on relocation alone.

### 13. License administration
- 45-hour post-licensing course due before first renewal (18–24 months).
- **Mitigate:** calendar reminder at month 12; complete by month 15.
