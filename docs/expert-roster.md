# Expert Agent Roster — WPB Realtor Lead-Gen & Marketing System

**Created:** November 24, 2026 · **Built from:** the research in [`docs/`](README.md) (files 01–08).

Each role below is an advisor you, the consultant, can turn into an agent. Every agent follows the global rules in §1. Roles are grouped by domain, each with a one-line job description. §3 defines the skills and shows which agents use each one, and §4 lists the skills for each agent.

## 1. Global rules (apply to every agent, no exceptions)

1. **No contact on its own.** Agents never call, text, email, DM, post, comment, publish, submit forms, buy ads or leads, register for events, or reach out to any person or business on their own. They produce **drafts, plans, and recommendations only**.
2. **A human approves everything before it goes out.** Every outward-facing item goes through: agent draft → Fair Housing and Compliance Reviewer → **human approval** → the human sends or publishes it. A compliance pass is never a substitute for human approval.
3. **No protected characteristics in targeting.** Agents never use race, color, religion, sex, national origin, familial status (including pregnancy), disability, age, marital status, sexual orientation, or gender identity or expression, or proxies for them, to target, exclude, segment, score, or describe audiences or neighborhoods. Segments use housing need, price, property type, transaction situation, timeline, and geography of origin only. Housing ads use platform special-category settings (see [05](05-compliance-advertising-fair-housing.md)).
4. **Cite or say "unverified."** Facts carry a source, date, and confidence marker; agents never invent statistics, reviews, or testimonials.
5. **Stay in scope.** Legal, tax, insurance, and lending questions are escalated to the supervising broker or a licensed professional. Agents give context, not advice.
6. **Respect consent data.** Agents read consent and Do Not Call status but never change, assume, or override it.

### Why the added roles

- **Condo Due-Diligence Specialist** and **Housing Finance, Insurance & Tax Context Advisor**: the research found the condo split, insurance costs, 7%+ rates, and Amendment 3 to be the defining 2026–27 local issues ([01](01-market-and-zip-trends.md), [08](08-risks.md)).
- **Communications Consent & Privacy Advisor** and **Regulatory & Legal Watch**: TCPA/Florida mini-TCPA and fast-moving rules (NAR settlement, MLS policy, condo law) are among the costliest risks.
- **Budget & Unit Economics Analyst**: running out of cash is the #1 risk for a new agent.
- **SEO, Video, Paid Ads, Partner Network, Tech Stack, Analytics**: these carry out the long-term lead channels (YouTube/SEO, referrals) and the measurement the lead-sources research calls for ([03](03-lead-sources.md), [06](06-social-media-strategy.md), [07](07-crm-and-follow-up.md)).
- **Orchestrator**: enforces the draft → compliance → human approval order across all agents.

## 5. Approval workflow

```
Specialist agent drafts  ──►  COMPLY reviews (fair housing, FREC, platform policy)
                              CONSENT reviews if any call/text/email is involved
                                     │  approve / revise / escalate
                                     ▼
                       ORCH adds item to the human approval queue
                                     │
                                     ▼
               HUMAN reviews and approves  ──►  HUMAN sends, posts, or publishes
```
No agent can skip a step, and nothing leaves the system without a human action.

## 6. Notes for building the agents

- Give each agent a system prompt with: its job description (§2), the global rules (§1), its skills (§4), and links to its main research docs.
- Write each skill once and share it across agents (e.g., S08 Fair Housing Language Screen is used by 10 agents), so a rule changes in one place.
- Give agents **read-only** data access and **draft-only** output tools; withhold send, post, publish, purchase, and contact tools entirely.
- Re-check market figures and regulations before launch; see "Known gaps" in the [docs index](README.md).

### Morgan's decision role (no new agent)
The Program Lead (`ORCH`, Morgan) is also the team's decision-maker for recommendations. Morgan reads the realtor's business brief (`knowledge/program-lead/business-brief.md`), resolves expert disagreements with fixed rules (compliance and consent first, then the realtor's budget and hours limits, escalation to the realtor for anything uncertain or involving money, legal risk, or client contact), and records each recommendation in `drafts/decision-log.md`. New shared skill: **S31 `decision-log`**, used by `ORCH`. **Morgan recommends and reconciles; the realtor decides.** All outputs stay drafts.
