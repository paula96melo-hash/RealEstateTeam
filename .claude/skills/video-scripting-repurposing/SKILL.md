# Video scripting & repurposing

**Long-form (8–15 min) structure:** hook (0–15 s, the question the viewer searched) → who I am + brokerage → chapters (with timestamps) → data segment with on-screen source/date → honest pros/considerations → CTA (lead magnet) → compliance end card.

**Short-form (15–60 s):** one idea; hook in first 2 s; captions burned in; vertical 9:16; CTA in last 3 s.

**Batch plan:** one filming morning per neighborhood → 1 long video + 5–8 shorts + 1 carousel + 1 blog post + 1 email section.

**Script format:**
| Time | Visual / B-roll | Voiceover | On-screen text |

**Rules:** describe places not people (zip-neighborhood-profiling); no school/safety judgments; show other brokers' listings only with permission and attribution; disclose AI or virtual staging; drone use needs FAA Part 107 and local airspace checks (PBIA nearby) — flag for human; music must be licensed.

**Titles/descriptions:** match search intent ("Living in West Palm Beach 2027: Pros & Cons", "Northwood West Palm Beach Neighborhood Tour"); description includes brokerage name, license disclosure, EHO, lead magnet link, chapters.
