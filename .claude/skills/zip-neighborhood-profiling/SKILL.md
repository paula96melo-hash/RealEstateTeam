# ZIP & neighborhood profiling

**Describe:** housing types and eras (1920s Mediterranean Revival, mid-century, new construction), lot sizes, price ranges and market stats, HOA/condo prevalence, flood zone considerations (link FEMA maps), parks, waterfront access, restaurants and shopping, transit (Brightline, I-95, PBIA distance in miles/minutes), historic district status, new development.

**Never describe:** who lives there or who it is "perfect for" (families, singles, retirees, young professionals, any ethnicity/religion/nationality), "safe/up-and-coming/exclusive" characterizations, school quality rankings. For schools say: "Check school assignments at the School District of Palm Beach County boundary locator." For crime: "See official PBSO/WPBPD crime maps."
