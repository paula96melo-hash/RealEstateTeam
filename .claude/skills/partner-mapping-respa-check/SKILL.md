# Partner mapping & RESPA check

**Partner types and value exchange:**
| Partner | Why | Value you give |
|---|---|---|
| Mortgage lenders | pre-approvals, DPA, workshops | education content, client service |
| Licensed insurance agents | quotes in inspection period | co-created insurance guide |
| Real estate / condo attorneys | contracts, condo docs, probate, divorce | referrals of clients who need them (no payment) |
| CPAs | Amendment 3, capital gains | seminar co-hosting |
| Condo/HOA managers | building info, assessments | market insight |
| Builders | new-construction co-op | represented buyers (register at first visit) |
| Out-of-state agents (NY/NJ/CT) | relocation referrals | licensed agent-to-agent referral fees (broker-to-broker) |

**RESPA §8 checks (settlement service providers):**
- [ ] No fee, kickback, gift, or "thing of value" in exchange for referrals
- [ ] Co-marketing costs split **in proportion** to each party's share of the ad/event
- [ ] No lender-paid CRM, ads, or leads in exchange for referrals
- [ ] Marketing service agreements → escalate to broker/attorney
- [ ] Referral fees only licensee-to-licensee through brokers (Florida)

**Output:** partner map (type, candidate criteria, outreach angle, value exchange, RESPA status) + relationship plan. Outreach messages are drafts for human approval; you never contact partners. Use public information only; don't compile personal data beyond business contact info.
