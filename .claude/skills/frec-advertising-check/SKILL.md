# FREC advertising check

1. **Brokerage licensed name** appears in the asset (profile bios, posts showing property or services, ads, email signatures, signs, cards, video, websites). Missing = FAIL.
2. **Licensee identifiable** — a reasonable person knows they are dealing with a licensee; agent name matches the license; a sales associate never appears to be the broker.
3. **Team rule (61J2-10.026)** — team name not larger than brokerage name; team name doesn't imply a separate brokerage (avoid "Realty", "Brokerage", "Agency", "Company", "Associates" — confirm current text); team operates under one broker with a designated responsible licensee.
4. **Not false, deceptive, or misleading** (also Fla. Stat. 475.42/475.25) — no unsubstantiated "#1/top/best"; no implying you sold a listing you didn't; just-sold/just-listed for others' listings only with listing broker permission and clear attribution; price/discount claims accurate.
5. **REALTOR®** — only if the agent is an NAR member; caps or ®; refers to membership, not a job title.
6. **Images** — virtual staging, AI-generated, or edited photos disclosed; MLS/IDX photos used per IDX rules and permissions.
7. **Brokerage policy** — note if broker pre-approval is required.

Output: checklist with ✅/❌ per item, exact fix for each ❌, and a reminder that `[Brokerage Name]` placeholders must be filled by the human. Rule text can change — cite `docs/05 §2` and suggest `regulatory-watch` confirm current flrules.org text for anything unusual.
