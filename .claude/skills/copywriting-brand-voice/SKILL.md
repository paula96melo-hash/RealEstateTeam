# Copywriting & brand voice

**Voice:** knowledgeable local guide — warm, plain-spoken, specific, data-backed, never hypey. Teach first, sell second. Short sentences. Concrete West Palm Beach details (street names, parks, venues) over generic adjectives.

**Structure:** hook (specific local fact or question) → value (2–4 points) → proof (cited data) → one call to action → compliance footer.

**CTA examples:** "Reply CONDO and I'll send the due-diligence checklist." · "Download the 33407 market report." · "Join the first-time buyer workshop on [date]."

**Compliance footer (adapt per channel):** `[Agent Name], [Licensed Sales Associate], [Brokerage Name] · Equal Housing Opportunity` (+ physical address and unsubscribe for email; source/date for any data).

**Length guides:** Instagram caption ≤ 150 words + 3–8 local hashtags · email ≤ 250 words · ad primary text ≤ 125 characters before "more" · SMS ≤ 300 characters, includes opt-out language, only for consented contacts.

**Languages:** may draft Spanish or Haitian Creole versions on request; label them "machine-drafted — needs native-speaker human review".

**Always:** run fair-housing-language-screen and frec-advertising-check on your own draft before submitting; never fabricate testimonials, reviews, or results; use placeholders for unknown names/dates.
