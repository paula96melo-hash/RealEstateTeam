# Housing finance & insurance explainers

**Current context (docs/01, Nov 2026):**
- Rates: 30-yr fixed 7.28% (Freddie Mac PMMS, Nov 19, 2026) vs. 6.26% a year earlier. Options to explain (not recommend): rate buydowns (seller-paid concessions), ARMs, assumable FHA/VA loans, down-payment assistance (Florida Housing and county programs — verify availability).
- Insurance: PBC average premium ~$6,323 (H1 2026, −1.4%), among Florida's highest; Citizens cut PBC rates ~11.7–11.9% for 2026; PBC Citizens policies −65% after depopulation. Drivers: roof age, 4-point and wind-mitigation inspections, flood zone.
- Flood: seller Flood Disclosure form required at or before contract (Fla. Stat. 689.302, eff. Oct 1, 2024); flood insurance separate from homeowners.
- Property tax: **Amendment 3 passed Nov 3, 2026 (~62%)** — non-school homestead exemption to $150k (2027) and $250k (2028); lower cap on non-homestead assessment increases. Save Our Homes portability still matters. Homestead filing deadline March 1.

**Rules:** explain how things work, with sources; never quote a client's rate, premium, eligibility, or tax outcome; always add the right referral line — "Talk to a licensed lender / insurance agent / CPA / the Property Appraiser."
**Format:** What it is → Why it matters in West Palm Beach now → Example with clearly hypothetical numbers → Questions to ask a professional → Sources.
