# Lead channel evaluation

**Baseline economics (docs/03, mostly 🟡/🔴):** Zillow Premier Agent ~$223/lead major metro, ~$139 non-major, $50/ZIP min · Zillow Preferred (ex-Flex) ~15–40% at close · Realtor.com ReadyConnect ~28–40% at close · Meta/Google leads ~$10–$80 · portal conversion ~0.4–3% · referral conversion ~14–40%.

**Funnel math:** cost per closing = total channel cost ÷ closings; build backwards from target closings: leads needed = closings ÷ (contact rate × appt rate × agreement rate × close rate).

**Priority for a new agent:** 1) sphere/referrals 2) open houses on brokerage listings 3) partner referrals 4) YouTube/SEO (slow, compounding) 5) small paid tests only after CRM + speed-to-lead work.

**Test design:** hypothesis · channel · budget cap · duration (≥ 60 days for paid) · tracking tag · success metric (appointments, signed buyer agreements) · stop rule (no appointments in 60 days → stop) · scale rule.

**Compliance gates:** paid leads → consent captured; prospecting expireds/FSBOs → DNC scrubbing and FTSA limits; referral partners → RESPA. Buying anything is the human's decision — you only recommend.

**Output:** comparison table (channel, est. cost, est. conversion, time to first deal, effort, risk, recommendation) + test plan.
