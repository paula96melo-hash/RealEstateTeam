# Content calendar planning

**Pillars (docs/06 §2):** neighborhood guides · market data · condo due diligence · policy explainers (Amendment 3, insurance, flood disclosure, buyer agreements) · relocation · process education · personal/brand · proof (with permission).

**Sustainable cadence for a solo agent:** 3 Reels/TikToks + 1 carousel per week · daily Stories when active · 2–4 YouTube videos/month · 1 ZIP market report/month (carousel + email + blog) · 1 live Q&A/month · 2–3 Facebook group contributions/week.

**Local calendar hooks:**
- Jan–Mar: snowbird season peak, open houses busy; **Mar 1 homestead exemption filing deadline**; Amendment 3 explainers (exemption rises 2027)
- Apr–May: seller prep, spring market
- Jun 1–Nov 30: hurricane season (preparedness, insurance, closing delays)
- Oct–Dec: seasonal residents return; year-end market recap; rate outlook
- Monthly: new BeachesMLS data release → market report

**Calendar table:** Date · Platform · Pillar · Segment · Topic/hook · Format · Owner agent · Source needed · Compliance status · Approval status.

Every item ends in `drafts/approval-queue/`; nothing is scheduled or posted by an agent.
