# Needs-based segmentation

**Allowed segmentation variables:** price band · property type (SF, condo, townhouse, new construction, 55+ HOPA-qualified community) · transaction situation (first purchase, move-up, downsizing, relocating from another market, investor, estate/probate, divorce, expired listing, FSBO, absentee owner, condo assessment exposure, insurance pressure) · timeline · financing status · target ZIPs/areas of interest · origin market for relocation (e.g., NY/NJ/CT metro) · stated interests in property features.

**Forbidden variables and proxies:** any protected class (federal, Florida, Palm Beach County — includes age, marital status, sexual orientation, gender identity/expression), and proxies: generational labels, "families", "retirees" as a targeting criterion, language as an exclusion filter, religious landmarks, school quality, "safe", nationality/ethnic media as exclusion.
- Offering content *in* Spanish or Haitian Creole is allowed (inclusive reach); excluding anyone by language is not.
- 55+ marketing is allowed only for HOPA-qualified communities, described as the community's legal status.

**Team segments (docs/02):** B1 Northeast/finance relocators · B2 first-time & move-up · B3 condo value/investor · B4 55+ communities & seasonal · B5 suburban SF/new construction · B6 second-home · B7 renters→owners · S1 condo owners facing assessments · S2 long-tenure homesteaders · S3 estate/probate/divorce · S4 out-of-state owners/landlords · S5 expireds · S6 FSBOs · S7 insurance-pressured owners.

**Output per segment:** need/trigger · price & property focus · areas of interest · pain points · channels · message angle · consent requirements · fit score (volume, value, competition, new-agent fit). Run any persona text through the fair-housing-language-screen skill.
