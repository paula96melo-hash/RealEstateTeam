# Compliant ad targeting

**Meta (Facebook/Instagram)**
- Declare **Special Ad Category: Housing** (country: US) for any ad about listings, buying/selling, home valuation, mortgage, or real estate services.
- Allowed: location with Meta's minimum radius, broad audience, Advantage+ settings Meta permits under the category, retargeting only as the category allows.
- Not allowed: age/gender targeting, ZIP-level targeting, detailed demographic/interest targeting that the category removes, Saved Audiences, lookalike-style or exclusion audiences built around protected classes.

**Google Ads (US housing policy):** no targeting by age, gender, parental status, marital status, or ZIP code; use location radius and keywords.

**TikTok, LinkedIn, Nextdoor:** apply each platform's housing special-category rules; if unclear, mark "verify platform policy".

**Plan spec (draft only — the human builds and launches):** objective · special category declared · geography (radius around [place]) · creative + copy (passed fair-housing and FREC screens) · lead form with consent language (see consent-dnc-verification) · budget & duration · success metric (cost per appointment) · stop rule.

**Never:** custom audiences from contact lists segmented by protected traits; language or interest choices meant to include/exclude a group; ad copy describing the people who "belong". Every ad plan goes to `compliance-reviewer`.
