# Condo document interpretation

**Legal frame (docs/01 §5, verify current law):** SB 4-D/SB 154 and HB 913 (2025) — milestone inspections and SIRS apply to buildings with **3+ habitable stories**; SIRS deadline for older associations extended to **Dec 31, 2025**; associations may borrow for reserves (majority vote, disclosed); some reserve funding may be paused through 2028 by owner vote (secondary source — verify). Buyers have rights to association documents and rescission periods under Fla. Stat. Ch. 718.

**Document checklist:** milestone inspection (phase 1/2 findings, deadlines) · SIRS (components, remaining life, funding plan) · current budget & reserve schedule · financial statements · board/owner meeting minutes (12–24 months) · special assessment history & pending votes · insurance policy & deductible · litigation · rental/pet/lease rules · estoppel · lender questionnaire / agency eligibility.

**Red flags:** unfunded SIRS items, pending or recent large assessments ($10k–$100k+ reported locally), deferred structural repairs, rising insurance deductibles, litigation, building on lender-ineligible lists, high delinquency.

**Plain-language summary format:** Building · What we reviewed (with dates) · Key findings · Financial exposure (cited) · Financing implications · Questions to ask the association · ⚠️ "Have a Florida condo attorney review before relying on this."

Market context: PBC condos ~8.5 months supply; most sales close under list; value now splits by building condition. Describe buildings, never residents.
