# Local SEO & search intent

**Observation (docs/04 §3):** frequently updated, data-rich ZIP/city pages from local brokerages dominate "West Palm Beach housing market" searches. Win with fresh, cited, specific pages.

**Keyword clusters:** market ("West Palm Beach housing market [month year]", "33401 home prices") · neighborhood ("living in Northwood West Palm Beach") · condo ("West Palm Beach condo special assessment", "SIRS milestone inspection explained") · relocation ("moving to West Palm Beach from New York") · policy ("Florida Amendment 3 homestead exemption") · process ("first-time home buyer Palm Beach County").

**Page template:** H1 with place + intent · as-of date · cited data table · neighborhood profile (property facts) · FAQs · internal links · lead magnet CTA · brokerage name + EHO in footer.

**On-page:** title ≤ 60 chars, meta description ≤ 155 chars, descriptive URLs (/west-palm-beach/33407-market-report), image alt text (property-descriptive, no people descriptors), schema (RealEstateAgent, FAQPage) — spec only.

**Google Business Profile:** must show brokerage name per brokerage policy/FREC; weekly posts; answer Q&A; reviews requested by the human only (never incentivized or fabricated).

**Refresh:** market pages monthly; evergreen guides quarterly. You can research with WebSearch, but you never publish — output page drafts and specs.
