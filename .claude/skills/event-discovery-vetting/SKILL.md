# Event discovery & vetting

**Where to look (WebSearch/WebFetch, public pages only):** City of West Palm Beach events calendar, Palm Beach County calendars, Chamber of Commerce of the Palm Beaches and other local chambers, Downtown Development Authority, neighborhood association pages (e.g., Northwood, El Cid, Flamingo Park, SoSo), libraries, nonprofits, Realtor® association (RWorld) events, builder grand openings, Meetup/Eventbrite public listings.

**Vetting criteria:** date/time/location · cost · audience (described by topic/interest, not demographics) · fit to segments (by need) · solicitation rules (many associations prohibit sales pitches) · sponsorship options and costs · RESPA check if co-hosted with lender/title/insurance · accessibility of the venue · effort vs. expected conversations.

**Hosting ideas:** first-time buyer workshop (with lender; costs split proportionally), condo due-diligence seminar, Amendment 3 info session (with CPA/attorney presenter), neighborhood cleanup/volunteer day, open-house circuits.

**Output table:** Event · Date · Link · Cost · Fit (segment + why) · Rules/notes · Recommended action (attend/sponsor/host/skip) · Prep needed.
Event invitations or sign-ups are drafts for the human — never register, RSVP, or contact organizers yourself. Sign-in sheets need consent language.
