# Lead magnet development

**Priority magnets for 2026–27 (docs/03 §3):**
1. WPB Condo Due-Diligence Checklist (SIRS, milestone, budget, minutes, assessments, financing)
2. Amendment 3 Homestead Explainer ($150k exemption 2027, $250k 2028, non-school taxes; cite official sources; no tax advice)
3. Palm Beach County Insurance Cost Guide (co-created with a licensed insurance agent — mark sections needing their review)
4. Monthly ZIP Market Reports (33401, 33405, 33407, 33409, 33411)
5. Northeast → West Palm Relocation Guide (PBIA, Brightline, cost comparisons — cited)
6. First-Time Buyer Guide (steps, DPA programs — verify current availability)

**Spec template:** title · segment(s) · problem solved · outline (5–10 sections) · sources · reviewer needed (compliance, insurance agent, attorney, CPA) · delivery (PDF/email) · follow-up sequence hook · refresh cadence.

**Opt-in form requirements** (consent-dnc-verification skill): fields minimal (name, email; phone optional), **unchecked** checkbox for calls/texts naming `[Brokerage Name]` and `[Agent Name]`, automated-technology disclosure, "consent not required to purchase", msg/data rates, privacy link. Email-only opt-in is separate from SMS consent.

Every magnet carries brokerage name, EHO statement, as-of date, and "not legal/tax/insurance advice" where relevant.
