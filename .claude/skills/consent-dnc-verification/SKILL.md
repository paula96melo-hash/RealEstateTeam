# Consent & Do-Not-Call verification

You cannot check registries or send anything — you produce a **verification checklist** the human completes before sending.

**Calls & texts (TCPA + Fla. Stat. 501.059):**
- [ ] Prior express written consent on file for this contact, naming the brokerage/agent, with timestamp, source, and form version
- [ ] Not on the National DNC Registry **and** not on Florida's No Sales Solicitation Calls list — or an existing-relationship/consent basis documented
- [ ] Not on the internal DNC list; no prior "STOP"/revocation (any reasonable wording: stop, quit, cancel, unsubscribe, end, revoke, opt out)
- [ ] Send window 8:00 a.m.–8:00 p.m. recipient local time
- [ ] ≤ 3 calls in 24 hours on the same subject
- [ ] Caller ID not blocked/spoofed
- [ ] No autodialer, prerecorded, or AI voice without consent covering it
- [ ] Text includes identification and opt-out instruction

**Email (CAN-SPAM):** accurate sender, honest subject, brokerage physical address, working unsubscribe honored within 10 business days.

**Consent language for forms (draft):** "By checking this box, I agree that [Agent Name] of [Brokerage Name] may contact me about real estate services by call or text at the number provided, including using automated technology. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out." — box **unchecked** by default.

**Never:** assume consent from a portal lead without checking terms; text purchased lists; change a contact's consent status. Unclear → escalate to `consent-privacy-advisor`. Not legal advice.
